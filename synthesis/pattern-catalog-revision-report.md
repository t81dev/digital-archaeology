# Pattern Catalog Revision Report: Recent Inclusions

> **A detailed report documenting the promotion of new architectural patterns, the refinement of existing core patterns, and the meta-conclusions derived from alternative computing paradigms.**

---

## 1. Meta-Conclusion

> **The recent inclusions revise the Digital Archaeology pattern catalog primarily by adding four highly portable architectural patterns around boundaries, representations, and permissions, showing that the long-term survival of alternative computing models depends on managing the [Interface / Conversion Tax](../patterns/interface-conversion-tax.md) and successfully hybridizing within dominant commodity platforms.**

By analyzing the Residue Number System (RNS), Logarithmic Number System (LNS), [Fluidic Logic](../GLOSSARY.md), [KeyKOS](../GLOSSARY.md)-style object capabilities, and Prolog/WAM/FGCS logic-programming hardware, the project has moved from isolated case studies to unified, durable instruments. These architectures demonstrate that when a paradigm departs from the dominant *positional-binary / ambient-authority / von Neumann / electronic-digital* mainstream, it experiences highly regular and predictable systemic forces.

---

## 2. Core Revisions and Admitted Patterns

We have formally promoted four candidate observations into standalone, reusable project patterns under `patterns/`:

### A. [Interface / Conversion Tax](../patterns/interface-conversion-tax.md) (`patterns/interface-conversion-tax.md`)
*   **Mechanism**: The performance, resource, or security penalty paid when converting values, authority, signals, or program representations from an alternative computational abstraction to interface with the dominant mainstream platform.
*   **Significance**: Explains why localized technical advantages (such as 1-cycle logarithmic multiplication) often fail to produce general-purpose displacement. It determines niche boundaries.

### B. [Abstract Machine Persistence](../patterns/abstract-machine-persistence.md) (`patterns/abstract-machine-persistence.md`)
*   **Mechanism**: Decoupling a high-level execution model from specialized hardware by compiling it to an elegant, software-defined virtual machine, which rides the commodity silicon performance curve.
*   **Significance**: WAM outlived specialized microcoded logic computers; [KeyKOS](../GLOSSARY.md) capability patterns migrated to WebAssembly and WASI runtimes.

### C. [Operator-Cost Inversion](../patterns/operator-cost-inversion.md) (`patterns/operator-cost-inversion.md`)
*   **Mechanism**: Shifting underlying mathematical, symbolic, or logical representation to invert relative operator complexity—making higher-order operations (multiplication, division) extremely cheap, while shifting complexity into previously trivial operations (addition, comparison).
*   **Significance**: Essential lever for co-designing domain-specific accelerators (such as NPU logarithmic tensor cores and RNS-based cryptographic engines).

### D. [Explicit Authority Substrate](../patterns/explicit-authority-substrate.md) (`patterns/explicit-authority-substrate.md`)
*   **Mechanism**: Merging designation and permission into unforgeable capability tokens (keys or descriptors) to enforce fine-grained, self-documenting, and least-authority isolation.
*   **Significance**: Maps the trade-offs of microsegmentation ([KeyKOS](../GLOSSARY.md), CHERI, WASI) against language bindings and ecosystem-compatibility friction.

## 3. Existing Patterns Refined & Strengthened

Six existing patterns have been updated to integrate new cross-lineage evidence:

1.  **[Ecosystem Lock-In](../patterns/ecosystem-lockin.md)**: Expanded to show how standard compiler and language assumptions (weighted-positional and ambient-authority structures) actively lock out non-standard representations (RNS) and protection models ([KeyKOS](../GLOSSARY.md)).
2.  **[Constraint Migration](../patterns/constraint-migration.md)**: Updated with the **Interconnect Wall** and **Security Wall**, illustrating how carry-free modular channels, low-bit logarithmic tensors, and CHERI register capability checks are revived due to modern sub-5nm fabrication limits.
3.  **[Heterogeneous Revival](../patterns/heterogeneous-revival.md)**: Refined to incorporate **Hybridization as the Primary Survival Mode** (Candidate B), demonstrating that sidelined abstractions (RNS-binary cryptographic multipliers, LNS-NPU tensor cores, soft-robotic fluidic manifolds, and CHERI spatial security registers) survive strictly in symbiosis with general-purpose CPU hosts.
4.  **[Economic Failures](../patterns/economic-failures.md)**: Added specific analyses of Czechoslovakia's EPOS RNS computer, standalone LNS chips, pure digital fluidic computers (FLUIDIC-1), and custom sequential logic workstations (PSI/PIM).
5.  **[Forgotten Abstractions](../patterns/forgotten-abstractions.md)**: Integrated several newly stabilized abstractions, including unforgeable capabilities, carry-free modular channels, log-domain cost inversion, non-moving fluidic switching, and logical [unification](../GLOSSARY.md).
6.  **[Recurring Ideas](../patterns/recurring-ideas.md)**: Strengthened with modern manifestations of alternative number systems (LNS8/FP8) and continuous orthogonal persistence ([KeyKOS](../GLOSSARY.md)) over persistent memory systems.

## 4. Evaluation of Rejected / Deferred Candidates

*   **Candidate B (Hybridization as Survival Mode)**: *Rejected as a standalone pattern; merged into [Heterogeneous Revival](../patterns/heterogeneous-revival.md).*
    *   *Rationale*: "Hybridization as Survival Mode" is the exact functional manifestation of *[Heterogeneous Revival](../patterns/heterogeneous-revival.md)*. Creating a separate document would produce unnecessary redundancy. Instead, `heterogeneous-revival.md` was expanded with a dedicated section on the *hybrid survival imperative*, consolidating the core thesis.
*   **Candidate F (Static Flow and Medium Leakage)**: *Deferred to local synthesis observations.*
    *   *Rationale*: While the constant venting power leakage of pure [fluidic logic](../GLOSSARY.md) is highly analogous to sub-nanometer CMOS static leakage, this phenomenon is heavily dependent on specific physical substrates and does not possess enough general software portability to warrant promotion to a global project pattern. It remains as a localized note in `synthesis/recent-inclusions-crosscut.md`.
